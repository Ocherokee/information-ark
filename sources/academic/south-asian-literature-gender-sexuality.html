A comprehensive reading list compiled by two students (Vriddhi and Tay) studying the region of South Asia. This reading list has a particular focus on gender and sexuality within the region.

South Asian Histories

Cross Regional

  • Maps In History of South Asia
  • Digital South Asia Library
  • Reading Subaltern Studies in South Asia by David Ludden
  • Towards a Lyric History of India by Aamir Mufti
  • Windows into a revolution: ethnographies of Maoism in South Asia by Alpa Shah and Judith Pettigrew
  • Direct versus Indirect Colonial Rule in India: Long-term Consequences by Lakshmi Iyer
  • South Asian Open Archives (SAOA)

Pakistan

  • Pakistan's Little-Known Communist Movement by Akhtar Aasim Sajjad
  • CIA-ISI Jihadi "Frankenstein" Sows Chaos, Reaps Death
  • Ethnopolitics, Ethnic Conflict, and The State in Pakistan by Farhan Hanaf Siddiqi
  • Globalization and Pakistan's Dilemma of Development by Hassan N. Gardezi
  • Communism and Pakistan by Kamran Asdar Ali
  • The Marxist Punjabi Movement: Language and Literary Radicalism in Pakistan by Sara Kazmi
  • The Left in Pakistan: A Brief History by Farooq Suhleria

India

  • Inglorious Empire by Shashi Tharoor
  • An Era of Darkness by Shashi Tharoor
  • "Martial Races" and "Imperial Subjects": Violence and Governance In Colonial India, 1857-1914 by Gavin Rand
  • The Politics of Culture by Ajiaz Ahmad
  • Famine, Affluence, and Morality by Peter Singer
  • Nine Decades of the Communist Movement in India by K.N. Ramachandran
  • The Naxalite Rebellion
  • The Price of Land by Sanjoy Chakravorty

Bangladesh

  • Bangladesh Liberation War by Amit Ranjan
  • Bangladesh: The Trial of Henry Kissinger by Christopher Hitchens
  • Why Them? by Fariha Roisin
  • Siraj Sikder Archive

Nepal

  • Revolution In Nepal by Alastair Reith
  • Seeking State Power by Kiyoko Ogura

Sri Lanka

  • Sri Lanka: The Poisoned Legacy of British Colonial Rule
  • Sri Lanka In A Modern Age by Nira Wickramasinghe

Colonialism, Nationalism and Gender in South Asia

  • Nations In An Imperial Crucible by Mrinalini Sinha
  • Gender and Colonialism: Expansion or Migration? by Durba Ghosh
  • Caste, Widow-Remarriage, and the Reform of Popular Culture in Colonial Bengal by Sekhar Bandyopadhyay
  • Textile Prison: Discourse on Shame in the Attire of the Gentlewoman in Colonial Bengal by Himani Bannerji
  • Whatever Happened to the Vedic Dasi? Orientalism, Nationalism and a Script for the Past by Uma Chakravarti
  • Contentious Traditions: The Debate on Sati in Colonial India by Lata Mani
  • Mapping the Imperial Social Formation: A Modest Proposal for Feminist History by Mrinalini Sinha
  • Refashioning Mother India: Feminism and Nationalism in Late-Colonial India by Mrinalini Sinha
  • Conjugality and Capital: Gender, Families, and Property under Colonial Law in India by Mytheli Sreenivas

Postcolonial South Asian Feminist Literature

  • Feminism Without Borders by Chandra Talpade Mohanty
  • South Asian Feminisms edited by Ania Loomba
  • Colonialism/Postcolonialism by Ania Loomba
  • Feminism, Imperialism, and Orientalism and Challenging the 'Indian Woman' by Shirin Rai
  • Of The Neoliberal Feminist by Mehreen Kasana
  • Under Western Eyes by Chandra Talpade Mohanty
  • I Would Rather Be a Cyborg Than a Goddess by Jasbir Puar
  • Can the Subaltern Speak? by Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak

Caste, Gender, and Dalit Literature

  • The Complete Writings of B.R. Ambedkar (includes The Annihilation of Caste)
  • The Dalit Women's Movement in India: Dalit Mahila Samiti by Jahnvi Andharia
  • Dalit Panthers Manifesto
  • Gender, Caste, and the Imagination of Equality by Anupama Rao
  • Revitalizing Dalit Feminism by Smita M. Patil
  • Caste In India: Their Mechanism, Genesis, and Development by B.R. Ambedkar
  • Caste in Black and White: Dalit Identity and the Translation of African American Literature by Manan Desai

Gender and LGBTQ Identity and History

  • Homonationalism as an Assemblage by Jasbir Puar
  • Queer Sexuality: A Cultural Narrative of India's Historical Archive by Rohit K. Dasgupta
  • Pride Climbing Higher: Stories By LGBT People From Nepal by Gita Manandhar
  • Queer Times Queer Assemblages by Jasbir Puar
  • Captive Genders by Yasmeen Nair
  • Without a Trace: Sexuality and the Colonial Archive by Anjali Arondekar
  • British colonialism and the criminalization of homosexuality by Enze Han and Joseph O'Mahoney
  • The Hijras of India: Cultural and Individual Dimensions of an Institutionalised Third Gender Role by Serena Nanda

Partition Literature and Gender

  • Violence Against Women During The Partition of India by Arunima Dey
  • Borders and Boundaries: Women In India's Partition by Ritu Menon
  • 1947 Partition Archive
  • Partition, Pakistan, and South Asian History: In Search of a Narrative by David Gilmartin
  • Community and Violence: Recalling Partition by Gyanendra Pandey
  • Recovery, Rupture, Resistance: Indian State and Abduction of Women during Partition by Ritu Menon and Kamla Bhasin

Diaspora, Identity, and Material Experiences

  • The Long and Saggy Sari by Ania Loomba
  • Constructing an Imperfect Citizen-Subject: Globalization, National Security, and Violence Against South Asian Women by Sharmila Lodhia
  • Resituating Discourses of 'Whiteness' and 'Asianness' in Northern England by Jasbir Puar

South Asian Anti-Blackness and Blackness in South Asia

  • Complicating the Tale of "Two Indians": Mapping South Asian Complicity in White Settler Colonialism by Shaista Patel
  • Political Blackness and British Asians by Tariq Modood
  • Gandhi in South Africa by Paul F. Power

Colorism in South Asia

  • Colorism Among South Asians: Title VII and Skin Tone Discrimination by Taunya Lovell Banks
  • Colour of Life Achievements: Historical and Media Influence of Identity Formation Based on Skin Colour in South Asia by Nazia Hussein

Women and Gandhi

  • The Violence of Gandhi's Non-Violence by Ania Loomba
  • Construction and Reconstruction of Women in Gandhi by Sujata Patel
  • Gandhi's 'Fallen' Sisters: Difference and the National Body Politic by Ashwini Tambe
